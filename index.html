How to play & strategy
  • Points = distance × speed × multiplier. Over 250 km/h you get a 1.5× speed bonus; leading the race (P1) gives 1.25×.
  • Near miss: pass traffic close (without touching) → +150, combo up. Overtake a rival → +300.
  • Every near miss, overtake, drift and pickup raises your combo (up to x6). It resets after 4 s idle or on a crash.
  • Corners: at high speed the car is pushed wide. Lift or brake before tight bends; the track gets twistier the further you go.
  • Drift (SPACE): sharper turning in bends, charges Nitro and scores drift points when released.
  • Slipstream: tuck in behind a car for extra speed and nitro, then swing out to pass.
  • Watch orange blinkers: traffic changes lanes. Save nitro for straights; boosting into a bend puts you on the grass.
  • Time Attack: checkpoints every 2 km add time, but the bonus shrinks each time. Endless: no clock, 3 crashes and you're out.
  • Grass slows you hard and drains the combo timer. Gold coins +50, blue cans refill nitro.
